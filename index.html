What this is

Not a retreat
company.
A transformational
initiation space.

Sacred Journeys was not built from strategy. It was built from surrender. A Bufo dream, a name that appeared in it, and all the alignments after falling into place to make the first retreat six months later. The shift from force to flow is embedded in the DNA of this brand. It is not a concept. It is the origin story.

We create containers where people reconnect to their truth, dissolve unconscious patterns, and return home to themselves. Through sacred medicine, conscious community, and structured integration.

We are not selling ceremonies. We are facilitating remembrance.

Small capacity. Intentional curation. A shaman with nearly a decade of experience and over 500 ceremonies, trained in the Shipibo tradition in Peru. A living community that does not end when the retreat does.

This is initiation into your next chapter. Not a reset. Not a vacation. Not spiritual tourism.

The founder

Trevor Johnston

I did not build Sacred Journeys from strategy. I built it from a dream I could not shake and a calling I could not explain. That dream led me to Guadalajara, where I met Humberto. The team came together and six months later, we hosted our first retreat.

I am not the facilitator. I am the vision-holder, the container-builder, and a participant in ceremony alongside everyone else. I walk the path. That distinction matters.

My personal shift from force to flow is the transmission this brand is built on. I have lived the gap between external success and internal alignment. I built from the other side of it.

I do not present as a guru. I present as someone walking the same path, holding the container so others can do the same work.

Visionary  /  Curator  /  Participant  /  Not the facilitator
The container is held by

Three people. Each one essential to what happens before, during, and after.

Humberto Alcalá
Shaman & Ceremony Facilitator

Trained in the Peruvian jungle near Pucallpa under a traditional Shipibo shaman. Nearly a decade of experience. Over 500 ceremonies across Mexico, the United States, Europe, and South America. He left a conventional path to dedicate his full life to this work. He lives what he teaches. There has never been a bad ceremony with Humberto.

Aleck Urtaza
Integration Lead & Space Holder

Present at every retreat. The integration anchor. The grounding presence. The person community members know personally, have sat with in sound healing, have processed life with in breathwork. Where Humberto brings the ceremony, Aleck holds the field before, during, and long after. The container does not end when you leave. Aleck is where it continues.

Allie Schlener
Energetic Support & Intuitive Guide

Over 10 years exploring holistic wellness, energy work, Reiki, and intuitive healing. She bridges traditional medicine with the subtle art of spiritual connection. Allie holds space for deep remembrance, helping others reconnect to their inner wisdom. When she is in the container, the field deepens, and the feminine is present.

How the container works

Preparation

Seven days in Puerto Escondido. Sacred plant medicine with Humberto. Sound healing and space-holding with Aleck, Allie and Trevor. A small, curated group of people who were meant to be in the room together.

03

The ceremony reveals. Integration is where transformation stabilizes. Ongoing community support. A group that stays active. The work does not end when you return home. That is when it begins.
